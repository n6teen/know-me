## The problem

Institutional traders needed sophisticated order types beyond simple market and limit orders. Specifically, they required Repeat Order functionality for recurring trades and Iceberg Order features to execute large orders without revealing the full size to the market.

## How I approached it

I worked on both frontend and backend of the production trading platform. For the frontend, I developed intuitive interfaces for configuring these advanced order types. On the backend, I fixed bugs and ensured the order execution logic was reliable and performant.

## The outcome

Successfully shipped Repeat Order and Iceberg Order features to production, enabling institutional traders to execute their strategies more effectively while maintaining market discretion.
