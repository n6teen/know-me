## The problem

Financial advisors needed a tool to help clients understand their tax-saving options through mutual fund investments. The existing process was manual and time-consuming, making it difficult to present clear, actionable plans.

## How I approached it

I built a web application that simulates tax deductions via mutual fund investments. The app allows advisors to input client data, run simulations, and export professional plans for client presentations.

## The outcome

The application streamlined the tax planning process, enabling advisors to quickly generate and share investment recommendations with their clients.
