## The problem

During Thailand's 2026 Prime Minister election, citizens and media needed access to real-time election results as votes were counted nationwide. The challenge was building a system that could handle high traffic while displaying accurate, up-to-the-minute data.

## How I approached it

I built a live election dashboard that refreshes nationwide results in real time. The system was designed for reliability under high load, with efficient data updates to minimize latency and ensure users always saw the latest counts.

## The outcome

The platform successfully delivered real-time election results to users throughout the election night, providing a reliable source of information during a critical democratic moment.
