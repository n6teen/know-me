## The problem

Employers and workers needed a reliable platform to connect, manage contracts, and handle operational workflows efficiently. The existing solutions were fragmented and didn't address the full lifecycle of workforce management.

## How I approached it

I contributed extensively to both design and development, working across the entire product lifecycle. Starting from shaping user flows and UX/UI design, I then implemented frontend and backend features. This involved close collaboration with the product team to translate business requirements into technical solutions that served employers, workers, and operational teams.

## The outcome

The platform successfully connects employers with workers while handling the complexity of operational workflows. By working across the full stack, I helped ensure consistency between the user experience and the underlying systems.
